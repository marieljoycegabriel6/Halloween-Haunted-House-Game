Escape the Haunted House

Teacher's desk: change the characters, riddles and help words. Then press Play.

    Characters

    Change the name shown on the answer buttons.

    Riddles

    The game starts on 3F and goes down. Each riddle is one door. One sentence per line. Both teams get the same riddles.

    Help words

    These words are underlined in the riddles. Students tap them to see a picture and hear the word.

    Save and share

    Your changes save on this computer automatically. To move them to another computer, copy the backup code and paste it there.
